# Summary template

Send this as the last message of the interview. Replace each `<...>` field. Keep every section;
write `None.` in a section that has nothing.

```markdown
## Decisions

| # | Decision | Answer | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q<n> | <title> | <chosen answer> | <one line> |

## Assumptions

- <what you assumed without a decision, and why>

## Out of scope

- <what the interview did not cover and will not be done>

Confirm before I act.
```

- One table row per settled decision, in question order.
- List as assumptions the decisions the user did not answer before stopping.
