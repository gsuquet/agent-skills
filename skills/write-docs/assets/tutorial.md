# Tutorial template

Replace each `<...>` field with text that follows its rule. Keep every section, in this order.

````markdown
# Tutorial: <what the reader will have built or done at the end>

<2 sentences: what the reader builds, and what they can do afterwards>

Time: <minutes the whole tutorial takes, measured on a clean run> minutes.

## What you need

- <tool and version, account or access; nothing the reader must configure beyond this list>

## Step 1: <action>

<instruction, then the command>

```sh
<command>
```

<the output the reader sees, trimmed to the lines that show it worked>

<one sentence: what the reader now has>

## Step <N>: <action>

<same shape as step 1, for each further step>

## What you built

- <one bullet per skill or result the reader now has>

## Next steps

- <link to a how-to for real use, then to a concept doc for depth>
````

- One path, no choices: pick the option for the reader and keep the others out.
- Every step ends with a result the reader sees.
- Run the whole tutorial from a clean state before you deliver it.
